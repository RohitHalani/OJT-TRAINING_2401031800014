Our Story

From a Tapri
to Your Heart

It all started with a humble stall at the corner of Manek Chowk, Ahmedabad — a small tapri (roadside stall) where Ramesh Bhai served piping hot chai and crispy snacks at dawn.

Over 26 years, the recipes haven't changed. The same masala blends, the same hand-rolled puris, the same love poured into every serving. Only the crowd has grown — from neighbourhood regulars to food lovers across the city.

At TapriFood, we believe the best food doesn't come from fancy kitchens — it comes from honest hands, trusted recipes, and the kind of hunger only street food can satisfy.
